A converter that keeps your files on your device

VidTune converts audio and video between the formats people use most. Turn a screen recording into an MP3, shrink a WAV, make an iPhone clip play on any TV, or save a voice memo as MP3. Everything happens in your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue, and nothing to install.

Any device, any common format

Choose a file, pick MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4 or WebM, and set the quality. On a phone the controls are large and sit in one column. Audio files can become video too: an MP3 turned into an MP4 gets a plain dark screen with your audio, for places that only accept video.

Choosing quality

For audio, bitrate sets the balance between size and sound: 128 kbps for speech, 192 kbps for everyday listening, 320 kbps for the best MP3 quality. WAV keeps everything but files are large. For video, 360p keeps files small, 720p is a good default and 1080p is sharpest. See the guides for MP4 to MP3 and MOV to MP4.

Use it responsibly

Convert only media you own, that is in the public domain, or that you have permission to use. Protected or encrypted media can't be converted. See the terms and how it works.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers. More on the FAQ page.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No. VidTune converts files in your browser, so your audio and video stay on your device. The only thing downloaded is the conversion software itself, which is needed once for M4A, MP4 and WebM.
Which formats can I convert between?
You can convert audio or video files to MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4 or WebM. An audio file converted to MP4 or WebM becomes a video with a plain dark screen and your audio, handy where only video is accepted.
Is VidTune free?
Yes. The service is supported by advertising, so there is nothing to pay and no account to create.
Why is video conversion slower than audio?
Audio conversion is light and usually takes seconds. Video is re-encoded inside your browser, which is slower than desktop software, so long or high-resolution clips take longer.
Is there a file size limit?
Files up to 300 MB work in the browser. Very large or high-resolution files may run out of memory on phones, so shorter clips are safer.
Which MP3 bitrate should I pick?
128 kbps is fine for speech, 192 kbps suits everyday music, and 320 kbps is the highest quality MP3 offers. Higher bitrates make larger files.
