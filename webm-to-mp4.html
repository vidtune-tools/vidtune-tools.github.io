WebM to MP4

WebM is designed for browsers. Screen recorders and some web tools produce it, but many editors, TVs and older phones don't open it. MP4 is the format nearly everything understands.

Converting re-encodes the video, so choose 720p or 1080p to keep detail. Choosing a higher resolution than the original won't make it sharper.

Questions about WebM to MP4

Why convert WebM to MP4?
MP4 is supported by almost every device and editing program. WebM often isn't.
Does it keep the audio?
Yes. Audio is converted to AAC inside the MP4.
Is it free?
Yes, with no account and no upload.
