Privacy Policy

A plain-language summary of how data is handled.

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Summary

VidTune converts audio and video files in your browser. Your files are not uploaded to our servers. This page explains what we do collect, what third parties may collect, and your choices.

Your files

Files you convert are processed on your device, in your browser's memory. They are not sent to VidTune or to any server we control, and they are discarded when you close or reload the page.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your name. We may receive basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you visit, through our hosting provider's logs and any analytics we add later. If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can reply.

Cookies and local storage

VidTune stores one small setting in your browser (your light or dark theme choice). It does not identify you. Advertising partners may set their own cookies, described below.

Hosting and third-party resources

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses and other technical data under its own privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads. The conversion software that runs in your browser is served from this website.

Advertising

VidTune is supported by advertising and may show ads from Google AdSense or similar partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads or learn more at aboutads.info. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before ads that use cookies are shown.

Analytics

VidTune does not currently use analytics. If we add them, we will update this page.

Children

VidTune is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because we hold very little, the main way this applies is to emails you send us. Contact us at vidtunetools@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and show the date at the top.
