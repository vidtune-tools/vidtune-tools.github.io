M4A to MP3

M4A (AAC audio) is what iPhones use for voice memos and what many Apple apps export. Some players, cars and older devices expect MP3, so converting makes the file play everywhere.

Both formats are lossy. Converting M4A to MP3 re-encodes the audio, so choose 192 or 320 kbps to keep as much quality as possible. For speech, 128 kbps is plenty.

Questions about M4A to MP3

Why convert M4A to MP3?
MP3 plays on almost every device and app. M4A support is less universal outside Apple products.
Does it work with iPhone voice memos?
Yes. Save the memo to Files, then add it here.
Will quality drop?
A little, because both formats are lossy. Use 320 kbps to keep the most detail.
