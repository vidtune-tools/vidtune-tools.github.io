MOV to MP4

MOV is Apple's video container, used by iPhones and QuickTime. It plays well on Apple devices but less reliably on TVs, Android phones and some editors. MP4 (H.264 video with AAC audio) plays almost everywhere.

Which resolution?

  • 360p for small files.
  • 720p for a balance of clarity and size.
  • 1080p for the sharpest result. It's slower and the file is larger.

Video is converted inside your browser, so it's slower than desktop software. Short clips finish quickly; long or very high-resolution ones take time and a lot of memory.

Questions about MOV to MP4

Why won't my MOV play on my TV?
Many TVs and Android devices don't support every MOV variant. MP4 with H.264 is the most widely supported.
Will it keep 4K?
Output is capped at 1080p to keep conversion practical in a browser.
Is the video uploaded?
No. Conversion runs on your device.
