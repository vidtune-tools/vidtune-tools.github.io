MP3, M4A or WAV?

MP3 works everywhere. M4A (AAC) sounds a little better than MP3 at the same size and is native on Apple devices. WAV is uncompressed: best for editing, but about ten times larger than a 128 kbps MP3.

FormatBest forSize for 10 minutes
MP3 128 kbpsSpeech, small filesAbout 10 MB
MP3 320 kbpsBest-quality MP3About 24 MB
M4A 256 kbpsMusic on Apple devicesAbout 19 MB
WAVEditing and archivingAbout 100 MB

Questions about Audio converter

What's the difference between MP3 and M4A?
Both are compressed. M4A uses AAC, which usually sounds a little better at the same size.
When should I use WAV?
When you'll edit the audio. For listening, MP3 or M4A is smaller.
Does M4A conversion need a download?
Yes, a one-time engine download of about 30 MB. MP3 and WAV load instantly.
