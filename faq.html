Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the usual questions.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No. VidTune converts files in your browser, so your audio and video stay on your device. The only thing downloaded is the conversion software itself, which is needed once for M4A, MP4 and WebM.
Which formats can I convert between?
You can convert audio or video files to MP3, WAV, M4A, MP4 or WebM. An audio file converted to MP4 or WebM becomes a video with a plain dark screen and your audio, handy where only video is accepted.
Is VidTune free?
Yes. The service is supported by advertising, so there is nothing to pay and no account to create.
Why is video conversion slower than audio?
Audio conversion is light and usually takes seconds. Video is re-encoded inside your browser, which is slower than desktop software, so long or high-resolution clips take longer.
Is there a file size limit?
Files up to 300 MB work in the browser. Very large or high-resolution files may run out of memory on phones, so shorter clips are safer.
Which MP3 bitrate should I pick?
128 kbps is fine for speech, 192 kbps suits everyday music, and 320 kbps is the highest quality MP3 offers. Higher bitrates make larger files.
Do I need an account?
No.
Why are there ads?
Ads pay for hosting. They're labelled, kept away from the buttons, and never required to be clicked.
Can I convert several files at once?
Not yet. One file at a time.
Is it legal to convert files?
Converting files you own or have permission to use is fine. You're responsible for the content you convert and for respecting copyright.
