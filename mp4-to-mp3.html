Turn an MP4 into an MP3

An MP4 holds video and audio together. Converting to MP3 keeps only the sound, which is handy for lectures, interviews, screen recordings and music videos you made yourself. The MP3 is also far smaller than the video.

Which bitrate?

  • 128 kbps for speech and small files.
  • 192 kbps for everyday music.
  • 320 kbps for the best MP3 quality.

The MP3 can't sound better than the audio in the original video. If you plan to edit the audio, choose WAV instead. Converting a video that has no sound track will produce silence.

Questions about MP4 to MP3

How do I convert MP4 to MP3?
Add your MP4 file, keep MP3 selected, pick a bitrate and press Convert. Then press Download.
Is the MP4 uploaded?
No. The conversion happens in your browser and the file never leaves your device.
Will the MP3 sound as good as the video?
It will sound as good as the video's audio track allows. Choosing 320 kbps keeps the most detail.
Can I do this on my phone?
Yes. Short clips work well. Very large videos may run out of memory on some phones.
