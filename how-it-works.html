How it works

Your browser does the work. Here's what that means.

  1. Add a file. Choose one from your device or drop it in. VidTune reads its type, length and size.
  2. Choose a format and quality. Audio files can be turned into MP4 or WebM too, with a plain dark screen.
  3. Convert. Audio to MP3 or WAV uses a small encoder. M4A, MP4 and WebM use a full conversion engine that downloads once, about 30 MB, then runs locally.
  4. Preview and download. Play the result, then save it. Nothing is stored on a server.

What happens to your file

It never leaves your device. The conversion software is downloaded to your browser, and your file is processed in memory there. Closing the tab discards it. See the Privacy Policy.

How long it takes

MP3 and WAV usually take seconds. Video takes longer because it's re-encoded in the browser, so expect it to be slower than desktop software, especially for long or high-resolution files.
