How to turn an MP3 into a video

Some places only accept video. Here's how to wrap your audio in an MP4.

Many upload forms, editing apps and playlists only accept video files. If you have a song, podcast clip or voice recording, you can turn it into an MP4 so it can go there.

How it works

  1. Open the MP3 to MP4 converter.
  2. Choose your audio file.
  3. Select MP4 (or WebM) and a resolution. 720p is a good default.
  4. Tap Convert, preview it, then Download.

The result is a video with a plain dark screen and your audio. The resolution only sets the size of the blank frame, since there's no picture to sharpen.

Want a picture or text on screen?

Add the MP4 to a video editor and place an image or text over the dark screen. Starting from a ready-made MP4 means the audio is already in a format editors accept.

Tips

  • Use 360p for the smallest file, 720p for most uses.
  • Works with WAV, M4A and other audio too.
  • Only use audio you own or have permission to share.

Quick answers

Can I add album art?
Not here. The video uses a plain dark frame. Use a video editor to add artwork.
Which resolution should I choose?
720p works for most places. Use 360p if you want the smallest file.
Is my audio uploaded?
No. Conversion runs in your browser.
