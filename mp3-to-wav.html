When to use WAV

WAV is uncompressed, which makes it the safe choice for editing software, video editors and some hardware that doesn't read MP3. Files are large: about 10 MB per minute for stereo at 44.1 kHz.

Converting an MP3 to WAV doesn't restore detail the MP3 already discarded. It just stores what's there without compressing it again, so repeated edits and exports don't lose more quality.

Questions about MP3 to WAV

Does MP3 to WAV improve quality?
No. It preserves the MP3's quality without further loss, but can't bring back what was already removed.
Why is the WAV so big?
WAV is uncompressed. Expect about 10 MB per minute of stereo audio.
Is my audio uploaded?
No. It's converted in your browser.
