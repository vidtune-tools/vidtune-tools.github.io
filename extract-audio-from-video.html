How to extract audio from a video

Turn the sound of any video you own into an MP3, right in your browser.

Sometimes you only want the sound: a lecture to listen to on a walk, an interview you recorded, or the audio from a screen recording. Extracting it gives you a much smaller file that plays in any music app.

The quick way

  1. Open the MP4 to MP3 converter.
  2. Tap Choose a file. On an iPhone or iPad you can pick from Photo Library, Files or Take Photo or Video.
  3. Keep MP3 selected and pick a quality.
  4. Tap Convert, then Download.

The video is processed on your device and never uploaded.

Which quality should you choose?

  • 128 kbps is plenty for speech and gives about 1 MB per minute.
  • 192 kbps suits music on phone speakers and earbuds.
  • 320 kbps is the best MP3 quality, at about 2.4 MB per minute.

The MP3 can only be as good as the video's own audio, so choosing 320 kbps for a quiet phone recording won't improve it.

MP3, M4A or WAV?

MP3 plays everywhere. M4A (AAC) sounds slightly better at the same size and is native on Apple devices. WAV keeps everything but is about ten times larger than a 128 kbps MP3, so it's mainly for editing. See MP3 vs WAV vs M4A.

If it doesn't work

  • Very large videos can run out of memory on phones. Try a shorter clip or a computer.
  • A video with no sound track produces silence.
  • Protected or encrypted videos can't be converted.

Only extract audio from videos you own or have permission to use.

Quick answers

Can I extract audio on my iPhone?
Yes. Open the converter in Safari, choose the video from your Photo Library, and convert it to MP3.
Does the video get uploaded?
No. Conversion happens in your browser.
Which format keeps the best quality?
WAV keeps everything but is large. MP3 at 320 kbps is the best compact option.
