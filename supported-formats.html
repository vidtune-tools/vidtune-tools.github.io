Input files

Common audio and video files: MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM, MKV, AVI, 3GP, MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, Opus and FLAC. If your browser can't open a file itself, the conversion engine tries to read it.

Limits

  • Up to 300 MB per file.
  • Video output is capped at 1080p.
  • One file at a time.
  • Audio converted to MP4 or WebM gets a plain dark screen, not artwork or visuals.
  • Encrypted or copy-protected media can't be converted.

Limits depend on your device's memory and may vary.
